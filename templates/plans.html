Actuarial Formulation

How Estimated Benefit is Computed

In this academic project, the estimated total protective benefit is modeled as:

Estimated Benefit = (Annual Predicted Premium × Policy Duration in Years) × Benefit Factor Rate

Disclaimer: The benefit calculation is a project-defined illustrative metric representing simulated insurance maturity protection. It does not represent an official financial return or investment guarantee.
